**About**

California's SB 253 and similar EU rules require companies with over $1B in revenue to report emissions across their supply chains. That means their small business suppliers are now asked for emissions data, but tools like Watershed and Sweep are built for, and priced for, large corporations. EcoTrack gives small businesses a simple, affordable way to measure and report their emissions.

A student version is also available.
